A Lucide glyph inside a circular tinted badge — the app's category marker. Requires Lucide loaded on the page.

```jsx
<CategoryIcon category="shopping" />
<CategoryIcon category="transportation" size={40} />
<CategoryIcon icon="coffee" color="#F59E0B" />
```

Known categories: shopping, transportation, bills, food, healthcare, housing, entertainment, education, income. Override `icon`/`color` for anything else.
