Transient status message with semantic tone + leading icon. Calm, factual copy. Requires Lucide loaded.

```jsx
<Toast tone="income">Transaction saved.</Toast>
<Toast tone="expense">Couldn't save. Check your connection and try again.</Toast>
<Toast tone="warning">You're nearing your Groceries limit — 340₺ left.</Toast>
```

`tone`: info · income · expense · warning.
