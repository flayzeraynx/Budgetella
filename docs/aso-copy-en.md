### Description — max 4000 characters
**Budgetella — Your budget, in your pocket.**

Track monthly income, expenses, and spending habits in one app. No cloud lock-in, no ads, no third-party selling. Just you and your money.

— CORE FEATURES —

• **Add transactions in seconds**: Tap the + button, choose amount and category, done. Use voice or receipt scan to add even faster.

• **Smart category suggestions**: Common merchants are auto-categorized. Custom keyword rules let you train Budgetella on your own habits.

• **Monthly summary & trends**: See exactly where your money goes. Compare to last month, spot the categories pulling your budget — all at a glance.

• **Budget goals**: Set monthly limits per category. Get a heads-up before you hit the cap. Track savings goals and see your progress in beautiful charts.

• **Receipt scanning (Premium)**: Snap a photo of any receipt — Budgetella reads the total and date automatically. No manual entry.

• **Voice transactions (Premium)**: Say "Add $25 for groceries today" and let Budgetella log it. Hands-free budgeting, even on the go.

• **Budgi AI assistant (Premium)**: Weekly and monthly AI-powered insights. Which category is squeezing you, where can you save — Budgi tells you, in plain language.

• **Home screen widget + Apple Watch widget**: Your budget status always in sight.

• **Face ID / Touch ID lock**: Biometric protection for your financial data.

• **Siri Shortcuts**: "Hey Siri, log $50 grocery in Budgetella" — instant entry.

— PRIVACY FIRST —

Budgetella stores your data on-device. Cloud backup via Firebase is optional (Premium); off by default. No ads, no third-party tracking, no analytics resale.

— PREMIUM —

Try free for 7 days. Then $4.99/month or $39.99/year (33% off). Cancel anytime — no questions, no friction.
