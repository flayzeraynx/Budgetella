1. Data controller

This notice explains how personal data is processed in the Budgetella mobile app (the "App", iOS and Android) and on the budgetella.app website. For users in Türkiye it is the information notice required by Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data ("KVKK").

  • Data controller: Ozan Kılıç (individual developer)
  • Address: [ADRES]
  • E-mail: support@budgetella.app

In short: we do not connect to your bank, we do not sell your data, and we show no ads. What you enter is kept on your device and synced to your own account in the cloud.

2. Data we process

2.1 Account

  • Your e-mail address and the name you give.
  • Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication (Google); we never see or store it in plain text.
  • If you use Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In: the name, e-mail (Apple's private relay address if you choose it) and account identifier that provider passes to us.

2.2 Financial entries you type in

  • Transactions: amount, currency, date, category, note, recurrence.
  • Your categories and app settings.

These are stored on your device and synced, under your account, to Google's Cloud Firestore database. We do not collect bank account details, card numbers or open-banking data.

2.3 Notifications

  • If you allow notifications, your device's push token (Firebase Cloud Messaging / Apple Push) and platform are linked to your account.

2.4 Subscription and purchases

  • Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) takes the payment. We never receive your card or payment details.
  • On Android, the purchase token generated by Google Play, the product ID and the subscription status are verified on our server and saved to your account.
  • On iOS, subscription status is checked on your device through the App Store (StoreKit).
  • If you subscribed in the past through the old web app with Stripe, your Stripe customer and subscription identifiers may still be linked to your account. No new payments are taken on the web.

2.5 AI features (only with your consent)

  • The messages you write to Budgi and a summary of your spending (amounts and category totals).
  • On iOS, the receipt photo you take or pick for receipt scanning.

See section 5 for details.

2.6 Voice entry, camera, biometrics

  • Voice entry: your speech is turned into text by your device's speech recognition service (Apple on iOS; on Android the device's speech provider, usually Google), which may process the audio on its servers. We do not store audio.
  • Receipt scanning on Android: the text is read on the device (Google ML Kit); the photo is not sent to us.
  • Face ID / fingerprint: biometric data stays in the operating system and never reaches us.

2.7 Diagnostics and usage data (Android only)

  • The Android app includes Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Performance Monitoring and Google Analytics for Firebase: crash reports, performance measurements, an app-installation identifier, device model, OS and app version, and general usage events.
  • The iOS app does not include these tools.

2.8 Website

  • Firebase Hosting, which serves the site, processes standard server logs such as your IP address with each request.
  • Google Analytics loads only if you accept (see section 8).

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Creating your account, storing your entries and syncing them between your devices, verifying your subscription: entering into and performing the contract with you (KVKK Art. 5/2-c).
  • Sending the notifications you allowed: performance of the contract and the permission you gave on your device.
  • AI features (Budgi, insights, receipt scanning on iOS): your explicit consent (KVKK Art. 5/1), asked inside the app.
  • Analytics cookies on the website: your explicit consent, asked in the cookie notice.
  • Crash, performance and usage measurement on Android; security: legitimate interest in keeping the service working and secure (KVKK Art. 5/2-f).
  • Requests from authorities: legal obligation (KVKK Art. 5/2-ç).

We do not sell your data to ad networks or share it with third parties for marketing. The app contains no advertising SDK. We do not send newsletters or marketing e-mail.

4. Transfers and service providers

To provide the service, your data is processed by the providers below. Their servers are outside Türkiye, so your data is transferred abroad.

  • Google (Firebase): Authentication (account), Cloud Firestore (your entries), Cloud Messaging (notifications), Cloud Functions (purchase verification, account deletion), Hosting (website); on Android also Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring and Analytics.
  • Google (Gemini API): only for the AI features you consented to.
  • Google (Google Play, Google Sign-In, Google Analytics): Android purchases, signing in with a Google account and, if you accept, website analytics.
  • Apple: App Store purchases, Sign in with Apple, Apple Push notifications, speech recognition on iOS.

The providers' own privacy notices: Google, Firebase, Apple. We do not share your data with anyone else unless the law requires it.

5. AI features

AI features run on the Google Gemini API, and the app asks for your consent before first use. If you do not consent, nothing is sent and you can keep using the rest of the app.

  • Budgi chat: your message and a summary of your spending (amounts and category totals) are sent to the Gemini API.
  • Spending insights (iOS): your monthly spending summary is sent.
  • Receipt scanning (iOS): the receipt photo is sent to extract the amount and merchant.

Requests go straight from your device to Google over an encrypted connection; they do not pass through our servers. Google processes this data under its Gemini API terms. Your chat history is stored only on your device. We recommend not writing sensitive information such as identity or health details in messages or receipts.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Retention: your account and entries are kept until you delete your account.
  • Deleting your account: in the app, Settings → Delete Account. This removes the data on your device and your sign-in account; when the account is deleted, an automatic server process permanently deletes all cloud records belonging to it (transactions, categories, settings, push tokens). If you cannot access the app, you can also request deletion at support@budgetella.app.
  • Backups: deleted data may remain in Google's own backup systems for the limited period stated in Google's policies.
  • Purchase records: kept by Apple and Google under their own policies.
  • Export: you can save a copy of your entries as a file in the app with Settings → Data → Export.
  • Security: data travels between your device and Google's servers over an encrypted (TLS) connection; cloud records can be accessed only with your own account.

7. Your rights

Under Article 11 of KVKK you have the right to:

  • learn whether your personal data is processed,
  • request information about it if it has been processed,
  • learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used for that purpose,
  • know the third parties, in Türkiye or abroad, to whom it is transferred,
  • request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate,
  • request erasure or destruction under the conditions in Article 7 of KVKK,
  • request that correction and erasure be notified to third parties to whom the data was transferred,
  • object to a result against you arising from analysis of your data exclusively by automated systems,
  • claim compensation if you suffer damage from unlawful processing.

How to use them: send your request from the e-mail address registered on your account to support@budgetella.app, or in writing to the address above. We conclude requests within 30 days at no charge. You can withdraw any consent you gave (AI features, analytics cookies) at any time. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board. If you live elsewhere, you may also have rights under your local law; contact us the same way.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

  • Necessary: the site keeps settings such as your cookie choice in your browser's local storage. No cookie is set for this.
  • Analytics (only with your consent): if you choose "Accept", Google Analytics 4 loads and sets the _ga and _ga_* cookies; the pages you visit, approximate location, and device and browser information are sent to Google. If you choose "Decline", Google Analytics never loads and no cookie is set.
  • We use no advertising cookies. Fonts and scripts are served from the site's own server.
